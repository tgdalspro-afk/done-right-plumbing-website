About Done Right Plumbing

Your Neighbor.
Your Plumber.

Done Right Plumbing is an owner-operated plumbing company serving Thousand Oaks, Newbury Park, and the surrounding Conejo Valley. When you call, you get Ray Anderson — not a dispatcher, not a crew you've never met.

Ray has built his reputation one job at a time over more than two decades in the trade. He shows up when he says he will, explains exactly what's wrong, and charges a fair price for quality work. It's that simple — and apparently, that rare.

From routine repairs to complex installations, Ray brings the same care and craftsmanship to every home. That's why so many customers call him their go-to plumber — and why referrals are his greatest compliment.
